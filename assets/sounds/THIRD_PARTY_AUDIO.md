# Match audio sources

- `dice-shuffle.wav` uses Wuzzy’s [wooden dice recording, take 3](https://opengameart.org/content/wooden-dice-on-wodden-table-roll), released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The recording was trimmed, time-compressed, and level-adjusted for the roll animation.
- `reaction-duck.wav` uses Bert76’s [Ducks snatching](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ducks_snatching.ogg), released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Selected calls were filtered and timed to the throw and landing.
