VarsityScan.glb includes an adaptation of Infinite, 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite-Realities.
Source geometry and diffuse map: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r178/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith
Original work: https://www.triplegangers.com/
The scan is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Adaptations for this prototype: cropped bust, fitted neck, baked vertex colors and skinning to the Varsity skeleton.
